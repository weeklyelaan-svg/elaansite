Privacy Policy for Elaannews.in

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

At Elaannews.in (referred to as "we," "us," or "our"), we are committed to protecting the privacy of our visitors and readers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

By accessing or using Elaannews.in, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We use your data to provide and improve the service and will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several different types of information to provide and improve our news service to you:

  • Usage Data and Analytics: We automatically collect information when you visit the site, including your IP address, browser type, browser version, the pages you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages, and other diagnostic data.

  • Cookies and Tracking: We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our service and hold certain information to improve your experience.

  • Contact Information: If you choose to contact us via email or through a contact form, we may collect your name and email address to respond to your inquiries.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for various purposes, including:

  • Providing and maintaining the website: To ensure the site functions correctly and to deliver news content.

  • Improving user experience: To analyze how users interact with the site so we can make improvements.

  • Displaying advertisements: As a news channel, we rely on advertising. We use third-party ad networks (specifically Google AdSense) to display ads.

3. Use of Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

Elaannews.in uses Google AdSense to serve ads. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site.

  • DoubleClick DART Cookie: Google uses the DART cookie to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google ad and content network privacy policy.

  • How Ads Work: Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • Personalized vs. Non-Personalized: Our site may use personalized advertising. This means that the ads you see may be based on your interests or previous browsing history.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services here: Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Cookies

Cookies are files with small amounts of data which may include an anonymous unique identifier. We use cookies to:

  • Understand and save your preferences for future visits.

  • Compile aggregate data about site traffic and site interactions to offer better site experiences and tools in the future.

  • Help Google AdSense deliver relevant advertisements.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our service.

5. How to Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising

If you would like to opt-out of personalized advertising:

  • Google Ads Settings: You can manage and opt-out of Google’s personalized ads by visiting the Google Ads Settings.

  • Network Advertising Initiative: You can opt-out of many third-party ad networks by visiting the NAI opt-out page at Network Advertising Initiative.

  • Browser Settings: You can adjust your browser settings to block third-party cookies.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will retain and use your information to the extent necessary to comply with our legal obligations (for example, if we are required to retain your data to comply with applicable laws), resolve disputes, and enforce our legal agreements and policies.

7. Third-Party Services

Our site may contain links to other sites. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us. Therefore, we strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of these websites. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

8. User Rights (GDPR and CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your location, you may have certain data protection rights:

  • The right to access, update, or delete: You have the right to request copies of your personal data, to rectify inaccurate data, or to request deletion of your data.

  • The right to restriction or objection: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data, or to object to processing.

  • The right to data portability: You have the right to be provided with a copy of your data in a structured format.

If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "effective date" at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

10. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.
