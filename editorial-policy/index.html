EDITORIAL POLICY

Elaan News — elaannews.in

Last Updated: October 2026 Version: 1.0

Preamble

Elaan News is an independent digital news platform dedicated to delivering timely, accurate, fair, and responsible journalism to Hindi-speaking audiences across India and beyond. Headquartered in Latur, Maharashtra, we cover national, regional, political, social, and international news that matters to our readers.

This Editorial Policy serves as the guiding framework for all journalists, writers, editors, contributors, and content team members at Elaan News. The purpose of this document is to ensure that every piece of content published on elaannews.in meets the highest standards of journalistic integrity, ethical conduct, and legal compliance.

By following this policy, we reaffirm our commitment to our readers — to inform, not mislead; to report, not sensationalise; and to serve the public interest above all else.

Part 1: Our Core Values

1.1 Accuracy

Everything we publish must be grounded in verified facts. Before any news story is published, it must be confirmed through at least two independent and credible sources. Speculation, rumour, or unverified information will never be presented as established fact.

1.2 Fairness

Elaan News does not take sides with any political party, ideology, religion, caste, or community. Every subject will be presented from a balanced perspective. On controversial issues, all relevant sides will be heard and represented in our reporting.

1.3 Independence

We are an independent news platform. Our editorial decisions are free from the influence of advertisers, political pressure, commercial interests, or any external party. A clear and strict separation between advertising and editorial content will always be maintained.

1.4 Transparency

We are transparent with our readers. If a mistake is made, we acknowledge it and correct it promptly. We are open about our sources, methods, and editorial decisions wherever appropriate.

1.5 Accountability

We are accountable to our readers, to society, and to the law. We are committed to correcting our errors and resolving reader complaints in a timely and fair manner.

Part 2: News Gathering and Verification Process

2.1 Source Verification

Primary Sources (Preferred):

  • Official government press releases and government websites
  • Parliamentary and legislative records and proceedings
  • Court orders and judicial decisions
  • Government data (NITI Aayog, RBI, MOSPI, etc.)
  • Eyewitness accounts and on-ground reporting

Secondary Sources (Acceptable):

  • Reputed news agencies such as PTI, ANI, and UNI
  • Nationally and internationally recognised news organisations
  • Statements from subject-matter experts and academics
  • Peer-reviewed research papers and academic studies

Unacceptable Sources:

  • Anonymous social media posts
  • Unverified WhatsApp forwards or messages
  • Unknown blogs or unverified websites
  • One-sided statements without corroboration

2.2 The Two-Source Rule

Any significant fact or claim must be confirmed by at least two independent sources before publication. If only one source is available, this limitation will be clearly disclosed within the article.

2.3 Breaking News Protocol

In breaking news situations:

  • An initial report may be published based on available confirmed facts
  • The article will be clearly labelled as "Breaking News" or "Developing Story"
  • The article will be updated as new verified information becomes available
  • All updates will carry a clear timestamp
  • Speculation will never be presented as confirmed fact

Part 3: Editorial Standards

3.1 Headlines and Titles Policy

  • Headlines must be accurate, clear, and non-misleading
  • Clickbait headlines are strictly prohibited
  • Headlines must accurately represent the content of the article
  • Exaggerated or sensationalist language will be avoided
  • Question-based headlines will only be used when the article provides a clear answer

3.2 Language and Tone

  • Language must be simple, clear, and accessible to all readers
  • Hindi will be used in its standard and correct form across all content
  • Offensive, inflammatory, or hate-inducing language is strictly prohibited
  • Biased language targeting any religion, caste, gender, region, or community is not permitted
  • News writing will maintain a neutral and objective tone at all times

3.3 Photo and Media Policy

  • Only licensed, self-owned, or Creative Commons-permitted images will be used
  • All images will carry proper credits and captions
  • Images will not be misleadingly edited or cropped to distort context
  • AI-generated or synthetic images will be clearly labelled as such
  • Private photographs of individuals will not be published without their consent

3.4 AI-Generated Content Policy

Elaan News may use AI tools as assistants in the content creation process. However:

  • AI-generated content must be reviewed and verified by a human editor before publication
  • Content that is AI-generated or AI-assisted will carry an appropriate disclosure label
  • AI cannot replace human fact-checking — all factual verification must be done by humans
  • AI-generated images or videos will carry a clear "AI Generated" label

Part 4: Policy on Specific Topics

4.1 Political News

  • All political parties and leaders will receive equal and fair coverage
  • Elaan News will not promote or oppose any political party
  • Election coverage will follow the guidelines issued by the Election Commission of India
  • Political advertisements will be clearly distinguished from editorial content
  • Opinion and analysis pieces will always be clearly separated from news reporting

4.2 Communal and Sensitive Issues

  • Content that could inflame communal tensions will not be published
  • Discriminatory content based on religion, caste, or community is strictly prohibited
  • Reporting on riots or violence will be handled with extreme care and responsibility
  • Victims will not be identified by their religious or caste identity unless it is a central and verified fact of the story
  • Guidelines issued by the Press Council of India will be strictly followed

4.3 Crime and Court Reporting

  • An accused person will not be presumed guilty until convicted by a court of law
  • Appropriate terms such as "accused," "suspect," or "alleged" will always be used
  • The identity of minors involved in criminal cases will not be disclosed
  • The identity of sexual assault survivors will never be published, in compliance with the POCSO Act and applicable laws
  • Suicide reporting will follow the guidelines of the World Health Organization (WHO) and the Press Council of India

4.4 Health and Science News

  • Health-related information will only be sourced from certified medical and scientific authorities
  • No treatment, drug, or health claim will be published without expert verification
  • Misleading health information or promotion of unverified remedies is strictly prohibited
  • Scientific research will be reported carefully, with limitations and context clearly explained

4.5 Regional Coverage — Maharashtra and Latur

  • News from Maharashtra, and particularly the Latur region, will be given priority coverage
  • Local issues including agriculture, education, employment, and healthcare will receive dedicated attention
  • Reporting that holds local administration and elected representatives accountable will be actively encouraged

Part 5: Advertising and Sponsored Content Policy

5.1 Separation of Advertising and Editorial Content

  • There will always be a clear and visible distinction between advertising and editorial content
  • Advertisers cannot influence our editorial decisions in any way
  • No news story will be suppressed, altered, or shaped due to advertiser pressure

5.2 Sponsored Content

  • Sponsored articles will be clearly labelled as "Sponsored," "Advertisement," or "Paid Content"
  • Sponsored content must also meet our standards of accuracy and ethical conduct
  • Sponsored content containing false or misleading claims will not be published

5.3 Affiliate Links

  • If an article contains affiliate links, this will be clearly disclosed to readers
  • Affiliate relationships will not influence editorial decisions or content recommendations

Part 6: Corrections and Clarifications Policy

6.1 Error Correction Process

Elaan News is committed to acknowledging and correcting mistakes promptly:

  • Minor Errors (spelling, grammar, minor factual): The article will be corrected and a brief correction note will be added at the end of the article
  • Significant Errors (incorrect facts, misleading information): The article will be updated with a clear and prominent correction notice
  • Serious Errors (defamatory content, legally incorrect information): The article will be removed immediately and readers will be informed

6.2 Format of a Correction Notice

A correction notice will include:

  • What was incorrect
  • What the correct information is
  • The date the correction was made
  • How the error occurred (where appropriate)

6.3 How to Request a Correction

Readers can report any error through:

  • Email: weeklyelaan@gmail.com
  • Website: elaannews.in/contact-us/

All correction requests will be reviewed within 48 hours.

Part 7: Conflict of Interest Policy

7.1 For Journalists and Writers

  • Journalists will not report on subjects in which they have a personal, financial, or family interest
  • Any potential conflict of interest must be disclosed to the editor before assignment
  • Journalists will not simultaneously work for or be affiliated with any political party or organisation
  • Accepting gifts, free travel, or benefits from sources or subjects of coverage is prohibited (unless essential for reporting purposes and disclosed)

7.2 Editorial Independence

  • Editors will make all editorial decisions free from any external pressure
  • Owners or management will not interfere in day-to-day editorial decisions
  • No news story will be suppressed or altered due to pressure from advertisers or any other party

Part 8: Privacy and Ethics

8.1 Personal Privacy

  • The public actions of public figures are fair to report; however, their private lives will not be unnecessarily intruded upon
  • The privacy of ordinary citizens will be fully respected at all times
  • The identity of victims — particularly survivors of sexual assault or child abuse — will never be disclosed
  • The dignity of deceased individuals will be respected in all reporting

8.2 Protection of Children

  • In cases involving minors, their identity, photographs, and names will not be published
  • The provisions of the POCSO Act, 2012 will be strictly followed
  • Any sensitive content involving children must receive approval from a senior editor before publication

8.3 Mental Health and Suicide Reporting

  • Suicide reporting will follow the WHO's "Reporting on Suicide" guidelines
  • Detailed descriptions of methods of suicide will not be included in any report
  • All such articles will include mental health helpline numbers:
    • iCall: 9152987821
    • Vandrevala Foundation: 1860-2662-345

Part 9: Digital and Social Media Policy

9.1 Social Media Content

  • Only verified and published content will be shared on Elaan News's official social media accounts
  • The same ethical standards that apply to the website apply equally to all social media platforms
  • Team members will refrain from speaking on behalf of Elaan News on their personal social media accounts

9.2 Viral and Trending Content

  • Viral content will not be published without independent verification
  • Accuracy will always take priority over speed, even for trending topics
  • Fact-check articles will be clearly labelled with a "Fact Check" tag

9.3 SEO and Digital Publishing

  • Headlines and content will not be made misleading for the sake of SEO performance
  • Keyword stuffing and clickbait tactics are prohibited
  • Content quality will always take precedence over search engine optimisation

Part 12: Policy Review and Updates

This Editorial Policy is a living document. It will be reviewed and updated under the following circumstances:

  • When new laws or regulations come into effect
  • When journalistic standards or best practices evolve
  • When the website expands its coverage or launches new services
  • A minimum annual review will be conducted regardless of any changes

All updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

Part 13: Commitment to Our Readers

At Elaan News, we believe that an informed society is a stronger society. We are committed to:

  • ✅ Publishing only verified, fact-checked information
  • ✅ Correcting errors quickly and transparently
  • ✅ Treating all communities, religions, and political groups with equal fairness
  • ✅ Protecting the privacy and dignity of individuals
  • ✅ Maintaining complete independence from political and commercial influence
  • ✅ Upholding the highest standards of responsible digital journalism

We are grateful for the trust our readers place in us, and we take that responsibility seriously every single day.
