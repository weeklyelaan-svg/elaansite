4. Consent under the DPDP Act (Detailed)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, consent is one of the main legal grounds for processing personal data. We follow the strict requirements of the DPDP Act and DPDP Rules, 2025 for obtaining consent.

A. Requirements for Valid Consent
Consent must be:

  • Free – You are not forced or pressured to give consent. You can reject non-essential processing and still use the basic website.
  • Specific – Consent is taken for clearly defined purposes (for example, analytics or advertising) and not for vague or unlimited purposes.
  • Informed – Before or at the time of asking for consent, we provide a clear notice explaining what data is collected, for what purpose, and what rights you have.
  • Unconditional – Consent is not bundled with unrelated conditions.
  • Unambiguous – Consent is given through a clear affirmative action (such as clicking “Accept” or turning a toggle on). Pre-ticked boxes, silence, or inactivity are not treated as consent.

B. Notice Before Consent
Before or while seeking your consent, we provide a notice that includes:

  • The personal data being collected
  • The specific purpose of processing
  • Your rights under the DPDP Act
  • How you can exercise those rights
  • How you can withdraw consent
  • Our contact details and Grievance Officer details

The notice is given in clear and plain language.

C. How We Collect Consent

  • Through a cookie consent banner with options such as Accept All, Reject All, and Customize
  • Through unticked checkboxes on forms (for example, newsletter signup)
  • Through clear affirmative actions only

Non-essential cookies (analytics and advertising) remain blocked until you give consent.

D. Withdrawal of Consent
You can withdraw your consent at any time as easily as you gave it.
After withdrawal, we will stop processing your data for that purpose (unless we have another lawful ground to continue).
You can withdraw consent by:

  • Using the cookie settings on the website
  • Writing to us at weeklyelaan@gmail.com

E. Children’s Consent
We do not knowingly process personal data of children under 18 years without verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian. We do not engage in tracking or targeted advertising directed at children.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies – Required for the website to function. These do not require consent.
  • Functional Cookies – Remember your preferences.
  • Performance / Analytics Cookies – Help us understand website performance and usage.
  • Advertising / Targeting Cookies – Used to show relevant advertisements.

You can manage or withdraw consent for non-essential cookies through the consent banner or your browser settings.

6. Sharing of Personal Data

We share personal data only in limited situations:

  • With trusted service providers (hosting, analytics, advertising, email services) who process data on our behalf under contracts
  • With advertising partners for showing relevant ads
  • With law enforcement, courts, or government authorities when required by law
  • In case of a business transfer (merger, acquisition, or sale of assets), with appropriate notice where required

We do not sell your personal information in the traditional sense. However, under CCPA/CPRA, certain sharing for targeted advertising may be considered a “sale” or “sharing”. You have the right to opt out.

7. Your Rights

A. Rights under the DPDP Act (India)

  • Right to access a summary of your personal data
  • Right to correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure of data that is no longer necessary
  • Right to withdraw consent
  • Right to grievance redressal

B. Rights under GDPR (EU and UK users)

  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing
  • Rights related to automated decision-making
  • Right to withdraw consent
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

C. Rights under CCPA/CPRA (California residents)

  • Right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used and shared
  • Right to request deletion of personal information
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information
  • Right to limit the use of Sensitive Personal Information (if applicable)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information
California residents can opt out by contacting us at weeklyelaan@gmail.com or through any opt-out mechanism available on the website.

D. Rights under VCDPA (Virginia residents)

  • Right to confirm whether we process your personal data and to access it
  • Right to correct inaccuracies
  • Right to delete personal data
  • Right to obtain a copy of personal data in a portable format
  • Right to opt out of processing for targeted advertising, sale of personal data, or certain profiling

8. How to Exercise Your Rights

We will verify your request as required by law and respond within the applicable legal timelines.
